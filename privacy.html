Privacy policy

Family Budget is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • It reads the owner’s own spreadsheet and email in order to keep the household budget up to date.
  • That data stays in the owner’s own Google account. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone else.
  • No data is collected from visitors to this page or from any other person.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
